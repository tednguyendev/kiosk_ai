# Kopitiam Food Court at Tan Tock Seng Hospital (TTSH)

405 seats, 21 food stalls. Located within Tan Tock Seng Hospital, Singapore.
Diners can use the FairPrice Group app to purchase meals and enjoy 10% discount off regular-priced items plus Linkpoints redemption.

## Stall 1: Kopi Kiosk
- **Cuisine:** Drinks & Desserts
- Kopitiam Signature Breakfast Set ($3 union member before 11am), Super Saver Bao set, traditional kopi/teh, Yakult Juices, Ice Spin series (kopi, teh, milo)

## Stall 1: Ann Chin Popiah
- **Cuisine:** Popiah (Michelin Guide)
- Founded 1958 by Mr Lim Kam Chwee from Fujian. Famous for thin, fragrant, chewy popiah skin. Heritage recipe from Upper Thomson Road.

## Stall 2: Bread Junction
- **Cuisine:** Bread & Bakery
- **Muslim-friendly** (halal certification pending 3-5 months after opening)

## Stall 3: Kallang Airport Wanton Mee
- **Cuisine:** Wanton Noodle
- Started by 2 young Singaporeans during 2020 Covid lockdown. Recipe from old hawker selling since 1960. Signatures: Wanton Noodle, Dark Sauce Wanton Noodle, Crispy Fried Wantons. Known for delicious Char Siew, piquant chilli sauce, crispy pork lard, hand-wrapped wantons.

## Stall 4: Teochew Pig Organ Soup & Braised
- **Cuisine:** Teochew Cuisine
- Committed to preserving authentic Teochew flavours. Must-tries: Salted Vegetable Pig's Organ Soup, Teochew noodles, Chaoshan braised meat.

## Stall 5: Yuan Wei Su Yu
- **Cuisine:** Vegetarian
- Promotes healthy, affordable plant-based eating. Low Oil, Low Salt, No added MSG, fresh ingredients. Light on the body, kind to the earth.
- **Menu items:** Various vegetarian dishes (plant-based options daily)

## Stall 6: Ying Jia Steamed Fish
- **Cuisine:** Steamed Fish

- **Menu items:** Bean Sauce Seabass, Bean Sprout, Fish meat, HK steamed seabass, bean sauce mangrove fish, cai pu golden fish, cai pu mangrove fish, cai pu steamed seabass, egg, hk steamed golden fish, hk steamed mangrove jack, lala

## Stall 7: Xiang Chi Mian
- **Cuisine:** Bak Chor Mee (Mince Meat Noodle)
- Preserves authentic Singapore hawker Bak Chor Mee. Sauce brightened with sambal chilli, lightened by black vinegar acidity. Crispy pork lard adds richness. Toppings: tender pork slices, pork liver, meatball, minced meat, braised shiitake mushroom.
